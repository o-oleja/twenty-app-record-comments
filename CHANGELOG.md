# Changelog

All notable changes to this application are documented in this file.

## 0.1.0

- "Comments" tab on Company, Person and Opportunity record pages.
- Comments are posted under the name of the workspace member who writes them; authors can delete their own comments.
- Image attachments: images are stored as regular attachments of the record (visible in its Files tab) and shown as thumbnails in the comment. Click a thumbnail to expand it; close it with the X button, a click on the image or Escape.
- Translated into all 33 Twenty languages: tab, object and field names and the comment UI.
